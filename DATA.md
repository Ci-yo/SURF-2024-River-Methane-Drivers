# Data card

## Included table

`data/grime_curated.csv` contains 1,113 matched observations and 17 fields: site/date identifiers, CH₄/CO₂/N₂O concentrations and fluxes, measurement method, coordinates, elevation, slope, Strahler order, basin size and channel type.

Missing values are retained. One extreme N₂O concentration sentinel is visible in the source table; this project does not use N₂O in its public models.

## Provenance

The table is a project-specific merged subset of **GRiMeDB: the Global River Methane Database of concentrations and fluxes**:

- Stanley, E. H. et al. (2023), *Earth System Science Data*, 15, 2879–2926. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-15-2879-2023
- GRiMeDB v2 data package: https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/f48cdb77282598052349e969920356ef
- Rocher-Ros, G. et al. (2023), *Global methane emissions from rivers and streams*, Nature 621, 530–535. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06344-6
